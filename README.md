## 📌 Overview & Ecosystem

Welcome to **Awesome Customer Onboarding Platform** - a curated, comprehensive ecosystem guide to **Customer Onboarding Platforms**, **Implementation Project Management Tools**, **Mutual Action Plans (MAPs)**, **Digital Customer Portals**, and **Post-Sale Client Delivery Solutions**.

These tools empower Customer Success (CS), Implementation, Professional Services, and Solutions Engineering teams to:
- ⏱️ **Accelerate Time-to-Value (TTV)**: Standardize implementation journeys, automate repeatable milestone handoffs, and eliminate onboarding bottlenecks.
- 🤝 **Collaborative Mutual Action Plans (MAPs)**: Keep clients and internal teams aligned with shared timelines, transparent deliverable assignments, and live progress tracking.
- 🌐 **Branded Customer Portals & Rooms**: Provide client-facing digital workspaces for document exchange, embeddable tools, video walkthroughs, and status updates.
- 🔄 **Seamless Sales-to-CS Handoffs**: Automatically transfer account context, scope requirements, and contract deliverables directly from CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.).
- 📊 **Measure Onboarding Health & Adoption**: Monitor milestone velocity, customer engagement signals, and early product adoption to prevent churn before it happens.

## 🌟 Additional Open-Source Solutions & Architecture

Teams building custom customer onboarding infrastructure frequently combine modular open-source components:

1. **Internal Implementation Trackers**: Use **OpenProject**, **Plane**, or **Vikunja** for tracking internal professional services tasks, milestone dates, and Gantt schedules.
2. **Client-Facing Onboarding Portals**: Build lightweight, branded customer portals using open-source document & wiki tools (e.g., **Outline**, **Docmost**, or **Appsmith**) to share mutual action plans and deliverables.
3. **In-Product Adoption & Tours**: Embed **Driver.js** or **Intro.js** directly into web applications to guide new users through initial account setup steps.
4. **Automated Onboarding Workflows**: Orchestrate client communications, account provisioning, and CRM updates across systems using workflow engines like **n8n** or **Activepieces**.

Contributions from Customer Success leaders, Implementation Managers, Professional Services engineers, and open-source developers are welcome!

1. 🍴 **Fork the repository** on GitHub.
2. 🌿 **Create a feature branch**: `git checkout -b add-new-onboarding-tool`
3. 📝 **Add your entry** in `README.md` following the exact table/list format:
   - For SaaS tools: Include Platform Name, Website Link, Focus/Description, Starting Pricing, and specific Free Tier/Trial Limits.
   - For Open-Source projects: Include Repo Name, GitHub Link, Stargazers Star Badge, and Description.
4. 🚀 **Submit a Pull Request** with a concise explanation of the addition.

⭐ **Star the repo** if you find it helpful for your customer onboarding workflows!

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This repository is a **community-curated index** for informational and educational purposes — it does not constitute an endorsement or vendor recommendation.
- Customer onboarding platforms should be evaluated for customer-facing portal quality, mutual action plan support, integration depth (CRM, project tools), automation, reporting on time-to-value, and total cost of ownership.
- Pure open-source equivalents to dedicated onboarding SaaS tools are still emerging. Most successful open implementations combine project management, portal, and digital-adoption components rather than a single all-in-one open platform.
